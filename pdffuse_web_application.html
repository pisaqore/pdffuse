Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our browser-native processing.

Are my sensitive documents uploaded to any server?

No, absolutely not. PDFFuse operates entirely inside your web browser using JavaScript and client-side libraries. Your files are processed in your device's memory (RAM) and are never transmitted over the internet.

Is there a file size or daily document limit?

Unlike traditional SaaS tools that charge for large files, PDFFuse has no artificial limits. The only restriction is the amount of RAM available on your smartphone or computer.

Does PDFFuse work on mobile devices?

Yes! PDFFuse is designed with a mobile-first responsive architecture. You can easily merge, rotate, or convert PDFs directly from your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone.
