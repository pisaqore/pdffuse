Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about browser-native processing.

Are my documents uploaded to any server?

No. PDFFuse processes your files with JavaScript inside your browser, in your device's memory, and never transmits them. Note that the page itself loads its code libraries and fonts from public CDNs, which can see standard request data such as your IP address, but never your documents.

Is there a file size or daily document limit?

PDFFuse sets no limits of its own. The practical limit is the memory available on your phone or computer.

Does PDFFuse work on mobile devices?

Yes. The layout is responsive, so you can manage and convert PDFs from iOS or Android. In the Page Manager, use the arrow buttons on touch screens (drag and drop works with a mouse).

Can I process password-protected PDFs?

Not currently. Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked in your PDF viewer first.

Which characters can I use in a watermark?

Latin letters, numbers and common symbols. The Turkish letters ş, ğ, ı, İ are converted to their closest Latin equivalents (s, g, i, I). Other scripts such as Cyrillic, Arabic or CJK are not supported yet.
